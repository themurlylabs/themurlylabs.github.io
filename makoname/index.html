Your name, in Korean. Makoname turns your name into a Korean name that keeps its sound or its meaning — written in Hangul and Hanja, with a romanization you can say out loud.

Android · Coming 2027

What it does

  • Creates Korean names from your own name, by sound, by meaning, or in pure Korean
  • Shows the Hanja behind each syllable and what it means
  • Lets you pick Korean letters to build a name without an English name
  • Makes share cards and name-art images for your stories and feed

Your privacy

No account, no sign-up. The names you type are processed on your device and are not sent to us. Read the full Privacy Policy.
