Makoname Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This policy explains what information the Makoname app ("the app") handles, why, and the choices you have. The app is made by The Murly Labs (더멀리랩스), a sole proprietorship in the Republic of Korea ("we", "us").

The short version

  • There is no account and no sign-up.
  • The names and choices you enter are processed on your device. We do not receive them.
  • Names you save stay on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob and uses Google Firebase to measure usage and fix crashes. These services collect some device and usage information, described below.

1. Information you enter

To create a Korean name you may enter a name, choose a gender or style, or pick Korean letters. This information is used only on your device to generate results. It is not sent to our servers — the app has no servers of its own — and it is never included in analytics events.

Names you save to "My names", your theme choice and notification setting are stored locally on your device. You can delete saved names at any time in Settings → Clear my names, or remove everything by uninstalling the app.

2. Information collected by third-party services

The app uses the following Google services. Each collects information directly from your device under its own terms.

3. Device features

  • Listen (pronunciation): the app passes the Korean name to the text-to-speech engine installed on your device. That engine is provided by your device maker or Google and handles the text under its own policy.
  • Save image: share cards and name art are saved to your photo gallery only when you tap save.
  • Share: when you share, the image or text goes to the app you choose. That app's policy applies to what you share.
  • "Today's name" notification: created on your device. No data is sent to us to deliver it.

4. Your choices

  • Ad consent: if you are in a region where consent is required (such as the EEA, the UK or Switzerland), you will be asked for consent and can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Notifications: turn "Today's name" off in the app's settings or your device settings.
  • Local data: delete saved names in the app or uninstall the app.

5. Data retention

Information stored on your device stays there until you delete it. Information collected by Google services is kept according to the retention settings and policies of those services.

6. International transfers

Google may process information collected by its services in countries other than your own, including the United States, under the safeguards described in Google's policies.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, the Korean Personal Information Protection Act, or US state privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, and to object to its processing. Because the app does not send us your entered names and has no account, we generally do not hold information that identifies you. For data held by Google, you can use the controls in your Google and device settings. For any request or question, contact us at the email below.

8. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The "Last updated" date above shows when it was last revised. Significant changes will be noted in the app or on this page.
